## 0.1.0

First release.

- `Adrian.configure`, `identify`, `trackScreen`, `present`, `dismiss` and `reset`.
- Full-screen messenger in the workspace's brand colour, with forms, action buttons, chat flows, file uploads and ratings. It follows the keyboard and system bars edge to edge.
- `AdrianLauncherButton` with an unread badge.
- `AdrianListener` callbacks and `Adrian.unreadCount`, `primaryColor`, `latestMessage`, `isAvailable` and `isPresented`.
- Links, help center articles and downloads open outside the app.
- Recovers by itself when the web view's renderer process dies.
